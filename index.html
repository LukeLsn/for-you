To My Favorite Human

" Reading your words felt like a exhale I didn’t know I was holding in. If my name feels euphonious to you, then your heart feels like home to me. I’m sorry the "ache" found you tonight while I was asleep, but please know that even in my dreams, I carry the weight of how much you mean to me. You talk about wanting to be "perfect" and keeping things light, but I need you to know something: I don’t need the polished, plastic version of you. I love the version of you that is sentimental at midnight, the version that feels things deeply, and even the version that thinks she falls short. You aren’t a project to be perfected; you are a person to be known. You mentioned existentialism—the idea that we are responsible for the meaning we create. If the universe is as vast and uncertain as they say, then choosing you is the most "intentional" thing I have ever done. You aren't just a part of my day; you are the lens through which the day becomes worth looking at. Thank you for choosing to be vulnerable instead of hiding. Thank you for learning my language and making my world lighter. Thank you for being the person who makes me want to show up, too. You see me as the "coolest" or "smartest," but the truth is, I’m just a person who got incredibly lucky to be found by you in that "unknown" place. You haven't just touched my heart; you've settled into it. Rest well, and don't worry about being "better" for me. Just keep being you. That is more than enough. I’ll see you soon, and yes—I’m already smiling thinking about it."
