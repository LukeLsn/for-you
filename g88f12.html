Happy Girlfriend's Day, Ella

"Distance tests what is real, but choosing each other through every storm is what makes us forever."
Dearest Ella,

On this Girlfriend’s Day, I wanted to take a moment away from all the noise to tell you what’s really in my heart. Being miles apart isn’t easy—there are days when the physical distance feels heavy, and words through a screen don't always say what we truly feel. Yet, despite every obstacle distance throws at us, you continue to be my safest place.

I know we’ve had our fair share of misunderstandings along the way. There were moments when silence felt heavy, or when things got complicated and emotional. But what makes us *us* isn't the absence of conflict—it's the way we always find our way back to each other. Thank you for never giving up on us, and for constantly choosing me even when it would have been easier to walk away.

To be honest, I know how flawed I can be. Next to your warmth, your grace, and your beautiful heart, I often feel like I fall short. But despite all my imperfections and mistake-filled moments, you still choose to trust me. That trust is something I will never, ever take for granted: You are so much more than just my girlfriend; you are my home, my anchor, and my favorite decision every single day. I am so lucky to have you.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love. Distance is temporary, but my love and commitment to you aren't going anywhere.

Forever & Always Yours, Luke
