Feb 07: Seven Reasons

"Seven things about you that make my world a lot brighter."

01.The way you don't shy away from the 'big' questions.
02.How you can make me feel like you're sitting right next to me through a screen. You have a way of shrinking the miles.
03.Your 'sleepy voice' or the way you look when you've just woken up—completely real and completely you.
04.The way you listen. You don't just hear the words; you actually feel what I'm saying.
05.Your specific, genuine laugh that lights up everything.
06.How you see the world differently than anyone I've ever met. You notice the small details I usually miss.
07.The fact that out of everyone in the world, you choose me every single day. I never take that for granted.
